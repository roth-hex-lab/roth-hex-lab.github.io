### HEX @ MICCAI 2026

We are excited to be part of **MICCAI 2026**, where **Shiyu Li** will present our work on a **multi-camera AR guidance system for surgical instrument handling and assembly**.
The paper, *“Multi-Camera AR Guidance System for Surgical Instrument Handling and Assembly: Investigating Workload and Efficiency,”* presents a marker-free AR system that combines multi-camera 6D pose estimation with in-situ visualization on a head-mounted display. The system provides step-by-step assembly guidance and tooltip localization to support scrub nurses during surgical instrumentation.
In a user study with **29 scrub nurses**, the AR system reduced task completion time by **21.3%** compared with a paper manual, while also lowering perceived workload. The benefits were particularly pronounced for participants with less experience with the instrument set.
The work demonstrates the potential of **marker-free multi-camera AR** to improve efficiency and support clearer, less cognitively demanding workflows in surgical environments.

Authors: Shiyu Li, Julian Kreimeier, Hannah Schieber, Dirk Müller, Bernhard Kainz, Rüdiger von Eisenhart-Rothe, Daniel Roth
